<summary><b>Case studies</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; problem, approach, result</summary>
<br>

<details>
<summary>Credit Card Fraud Detection</summary>

- **Problem:** 284K+ transactions where fraud is a tiny share of the data. The goal was a scalable, auditable detection engine.
- **Approach:** A 7-stage PySpark pipeline with MLlib preprocessing. SMOTE and XGBoost class weighting handle the imbalance, and SHAP explains each prediction.
- **Result:** 0.98 AUC-ROC and 0.85 AUC-PR on heavily skewed data.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Swiggy & CRED Review Analysis</summary>

- **Problem:** When did users start getting unhappy, and what were they complaining about?
- **Approach:** VADER scored each review, PELT found changepoints in the sentiment series, and TF-IDF surfaced the top complaint themes. Mann-Whitney U and Cohen's d validated the drops.
- **Result:** A statistically significant 1.3-star rating decline across 25K+ reviews.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Fake News Detector</summary>

- **Problem:** Classify news articles as real or fake and serve the model as a live API.
- **Approach:** Fine-tuned DistilBERT on the 70K+ WELFake dataset, then wrapped it in FastAPI and Docker on Hugging Face Spaces.
- **Result:** 82% test accuracy with a public inference endpoint.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Job Recommendation Engine</summary>

- **Problem:** Match a raw resume to relevant job descriptions with instant feedback.
- **Approach:** TF-IDF vectorizes the resume and each description, cosine similarity ranks the matches, and Streamlit shows live scores.
- **Result:** Sub-second matching across a corpus of job descriptions.
</details>

<details>
<summary>FlipSatisfy Classifier</summary>

- **Problem:** Predict satisfaction from 85,907 Flipkart support messages early enough to flag escalations.
- **Approach:** TF-IDF features with Random Forest, AdaBoost and KNN. Every experiment was logged in MLflow and the final model was served with FastAPI in Docker.
- **Result:** Accuracy improved from 95.6% to 96.9% across tracked iterations.
</details>

</details>
